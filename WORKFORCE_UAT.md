# Workforce and Site Attendance UAT

## Purpose

Use this checklist to accept HR.1 through HR.4 on the seeded quarry tenant. Record failures with the user, URL, project, site, date, and screenshot.

## Preparation

1. Run php artisan migrate:fresh --seed.
2. Start the scheduler locally with php artisan schedule:work.
3. Use the seeded quarry tenant and confirm the current branch is selected.
4. Test desktop at approximately 1440 px wide and mobile at approximately 390 px wide.
5. Use lemi@gmail.com for administrator checks and luate@gmail.com for restricted-access checks.

## 1. Permissions

- Confirm a user without workforce.view receives 403 for /workforce and /workforce/attendance.
- Confirm a user without workforce.reports.view receives 403 for /workforce/exceptions.
- Confirm hidden buttons do not bypass server-side permissions when their URLs are requested directly.
- Confirm users see only projects, sites, staff, attendance, and exceptions within their tenant and branch access.

Pass when UI visibility and direct HTTP access enforce the same permissions.

## 2. Trades and Deployments

- Create a trade and confirm its code can be autogenerated and edited.
- Deactivate it and confirm it moves to the inactive tab and cannot be selected for new records.
- Assign a staff member to a project and site.
- Reassign the staff member and confirm the previous deployment is ended rather than overwritten.
- Open the audit trail and confirm the trade and deployment events are present.

Pass when deployment history is preserved and inactive reference data cannot be used for new work.

## 3. Attendance Capture

- Create separate day and night registers for the same site and date.
- Load deployed staff and use Mark all present.
- Change one worker to absent and confirm working hours become zero.
- Add a subcontractor crew using a registered subcontractor company and trade.
- Save a draft, close the page, reopen it, and confirm all lines remain.
- Repeat on mobile and confirm the page does not overflow horizontally and the main actions remain reachable.

Pass when attendance is quick to capture, valid on mobile, and unique by site, date, and shift.

## 4. Confirmation and Reopening

- Confirm a draft register and verify it becomes read-only.
- Attempt a direct update to the confirmed register and expect rejection.
- Reopen it with an authorized user and mandatory reason.
- Confirm the register becomes editable and the audit trail records both confirmation and reopening.
- Reconfirm it after correction.

Pass when confirmed attendance cannot be silently changed.

## 5. DSR Labour Comparison

- Open the seeded matched DSR and confirm attended and reported person-hours agree.
- Open the seeded under-reported DSR and confirm the unused attended hours are shown as a warning.
- Open the seeded approved over-reported DSR and confirm the approval snapshot and override reason are visible.
- Create an over-reported submitted DSR as a user without override permission and confirm approval is blocked.
- Approve with a user holding daily-site-reports.override-labour-variance, provide a reason, and confirm the reason appears in workflow history and audit trail.
- Submit a DSR with labour but no confirmed attendance and confirm submission remains allowed while the warning is visible.

Pass when attendance supports DSR review without preventing draft preparation or ordinary submission.

## 6. Exception Report

- Open /workforce/exceptions.
- Widen the From date to include the historical quarry DSR examples.
- Filter by project, site, From date, and To date.
- Confirm overdue attendance, missing attendance, under-reporting, over-reporting, and reopened-attendance rows appear only when applicable.
- Follow each Review record link and confirm it opens the correct attendance register or DSR.
- Confirm an approved DSR continues to show its stored approval snapshot even if attendance is later changed.

Pass when the report is scoped, actionable, and does not rewrite approved evidence.

## 7. Notifications

1. Run php artisan workforce:process-exceptions.
2. Confirm the overdue attendance recorder and appropriate site/project users receive an in-app warning.
3. Run the command again and confirm the same exception is not duplicated.
4. Submit a DSR with missing or excessive attendance and confirm reviewers receive a linked notification.
5. Reopen attendance supporting a submitted or approved DSR and confirm affected reviewers receive a critical notification.

Pass when every alert links to the affected record and repeated scheduled runs remain deduplicated.

## Acceptance

The workforce MVP is ready for pilot use when all sections pass on desktop and mobile, focused tests pass, PHPStan is clean, and the repository lint command completes without changes.
