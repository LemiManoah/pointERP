# PointERP Workforce and Site Attendance MVP

## 1. Purpose

This document defines a small, practical workforce and site-attendance module for PointERP. Its purpose is to answer three operational questions:

1. Who is assigned to each project and site?
2. Who attended work on a particular day?
3. Does the labour reported in the Daily Site Report reasonably agree with confirmed attendance?

This is not a payroll or full corporate HR system. The MVP supports site control, labour accountability and DSR review without introducing unnecessary HR administration.

The module may be scheduled as Phase 3F after the current Phase 3 completion and pilot-hardening work is accepted. `PROJECT_ROADMAP.md` remains the authority for programme sequencing.

## 2. Existing System to Reuse

The implementation must extend existing modules instead of creating competing records:

- `staff` remains the master record for company employees and individually registered casual workers.
- `staff_positions` remains the corporate job-title catalogue.
- `customers` of type `subcontractor` remain the master records for subcontracting companies.
- Existing projects, sites, branches and project/site access rules define operational scope.
- Existing DSR labour lines remain the record of labour applied to daily work.
- Existing permissions, policies, audit trail, notifications, confirmation dialogs and toasts must be reused.
- Existing document controls may hold workforce evidence where required.

An ERP user account remains optional. A worker may exist in `staff` without being able to log in.

## 3. MVP Boundaries

### Included

- Tenant-managed construction trades.
- Employment type and primary trade on staff records.
- Historical staff deployment to projects and sites.
- Daily site attendance by day or night shift.
- Individual attendance for company staff and casuals.
- Aggregate attendance for subcontractor labour by company and trade.
- Draft and confirmed attendance workflow.
- DSR labour selection from registered trades and subcontractor companies.
- Person-hour comparison between confirmed attendance and DSR labour.
- Permission-controlled explanation when DSR labour exceeds attendance.
- Audit events, basic exception reporting and demo seed data.

### Explicitly Deferred

- Payroll, payslips, PAYE, NSSF, loans and salary processing.
- Attendance-based worker payment or supplier liability creation.
- Leave requests, leave balances and public-holiday entitlement calculations.
- Camp allowances and site-rotation scheduling.
- Biometric, facial-recognition, GPS and access-control hardware.
- Employee appraisals, recruitment and disciplinary management.
- Detailed worker qualifications and certification expiry workflows.
- Signature capture and offline synchronization.
- Storing national ID or next-of-kin information until the client approves a privacy and retention policy.

## 4. Simple Operating Model

The MVP uses four concepts:

1. **Position** describes a corporate job, such as Site Engineer or Store Keeper.
2. **Trade** describes practical work capability, such as Mason, Welder or Excavator Operator.
3. **Deployment** says where a staff member is currently expected to work.
4. **Attendance** records who was actually present and for how many hours.

Employment type and trade are separate. For example, a permanent employee and a casual worker may both be Masons. An Equipment Operator is a trade, not an employment type.

Subcontractor companies are not Staff records. Their attendance is entered as an aggregate crew line linked to the existing subcontractor company.

## 5. Data Model

All new records use UUIDs and follow the existing tenant, branch, timestamp, audit and soft-delete conventions where appropriate. PHP enum classes hold controlled values; migrations store their string values.

### 5.1 `workforce_trades`

Tenant-managed master data:

- `id`
- `tenant_id`
- `code`, autogenerated from the name but editable
- `name`
- `category`: skilled, semi-skilled, unskilled or specialist
- `is_active`
- `created_by`, `updated_by`
- timestamps and soft deletes

Rules:

- Code is unique within a tenant.
- Inactive trades remain visible on historical records but cannot be selected for new records.
- Rates do not belong in this table in the MVP.

### 5.2 Extend `staff`

Add only:

- `employment_type`: permanent, fixed-term or casual
- `primary_trade_id`, nullable

Do not add `current_project_id` or `current_site_id`. Current placement is derived from deployments so history is retained.

### 5.3 `staff_deployments`

- `id`
- `tenant_id`, `branch_id`
- `staff_id`
- `project_id`
- `site_id`, nullable when deployed at project level
- `workforce_trade_id`, nullable override of the staff member's primary trade
- `starts_on`
- `ends_on`, nullable
- `status`: active or ended
- `notes`, nullable
- `assigned_by`, `ended_by`
- timestamps

Rules:

- A deployment must remain in the staff member's authorized tenant and branch scope.
- A staff member has one active primary deployment in the MVP.
- Reassignment ends the current deployment and creates another record; it does not overwrite history.
- Attendance may still include an authorized worker not yet deployed, but the system warns the recorder.

### 5.4 `site_attendance_registers`

- `id`
- `tenant_id`, `branch_id`
- `project_id`, `site_id`
- `attendance_date`
- `shift`: day or night
- `status`: draft or confirmed
- `notes`, nullable
- `recorded_by`
- `confirmed_by`, `confirmed_at`, nullable
- `reopened_by`, `reopened_at`, `reopen_reason`, nullable
- timestamps

Unique key: tenant, site, attendance date and shift.

Rules:

- Only one register exists for a site, date and shift.
- Draft registers are editable.
- Confirmed registers are read-only.
- A user with reopen permission may reopen a confirmed register with a mandatory reason.
- Reopening and reconfirming are fully audited.

### 5.5 `site_attendance_records`

- `id`
- `tenant_id`, `branch_id`
- `site_attendance_register_id`
- `staff_id`, nullable
- `subcontractor_id`, nullable
- `workforce_trade_id`
- `labour_source`: internal, casual or subcontractor
- `worker_name_snapshot`, nullable
- `subcontractor_name_snapshot`, nullable
- `headcount`, default 1
- `attendance_status`: present, absent, sick or authorized_absence
- `regular_hours_per_person`, default 8
- `overtime_hours_per_person`, default 0
- `notes`, nullable
- timestamps

Rules:

- A line represents either a registered staff member or a subcontractor crew, never both.
- Registered staff lines use `headcount = 1`.
- Subcontractor lines may use an aggregate headcount.
- Absent or excused lines must have zero working hours.
- Total person-hours are calculated, not stored as an editable value:

```text
headcount x (regular hours per person + overtime hours per person)
```

## 6. DSR Integration

### 6.1 Extend DSR Labour Lines

Add:

- `workforce_trade_id`, nullable for old historical records
- no single attendance-register foreign key: a daily DSR can cover both day and night shifts, so the comparison resolves every confirmed register for the site/date and snapshots their IDs during approval
- retain trade, subcontractor and rate snapshots already used by the DSR

New DSR labour entries must select:

- labour source;
- trade;
- subcontractor company when the source is subcontractor;
- headcount;
- hours per person;
- optional notes.

The system calculates DSR person-hours as:

```text
headcount x hours per person
```

### 6.2 Comparison Rule

When a confirmed attendance register exists for the DSR site, date and applicable shifts, compare attendance and DSR person-hours by:

- labour source;
- trade;
- subcontractor company where applicable.

Results:

- **Matched:** reported and attended person-hours agree within the configured tolerance.
- **Under-reported:** attendance exceeds DSR labour; show a warning because time may be unallocated.
- **Over-reported:** DSR labour exceeds attendance; block approval unless an authorized user provides an override reason.
- **Missing attendance:** show a warning but do not block the first MVP release.

Do not create a separate reconciliation workflow or reconciliation table. Calculate the comparison from attendance and DSR lines. On DSR approval, store a compact JSON comparison snapshot, result status, checked timestamp and any override reason on the DSR so the approved evidence cannot silently change.

### 6.3 Approval Behaviour

- Draft DSRs may be prepared before attendance is confirmed.
- Submission shows attendance warnings but remains possible.
- Approval reruns the comparison using confirmed attendance.
- Over-reporting blocks approval unless the actor has `daily-site-reports.override-labour-variance` and supplies a reason.
- The approval and override are recorded in both workflow history and the audit trail.
- Attendance does not create wages, expenses or payments.

## 7. User Workflows

### 7.1 Maintain Trades

An authorized administrator opens Trades, creates or edits a trade, and manages active/inactive records in separate tabs.

### 7.2 Prepare Workers

The existing Staff screen gains Employment Type and Primary Trade. Staff creation, user-account creation and staff positions otherwise remain unchanged.

### 7.3 Deploy Staff

An authorized manager selects a staff member, project, site, start date and trade. Reassignment ends the previous deployment and creates the new deployment.

### 7.4 Record Attendance

The foreman or site engineer:

1. Selects the site, date and shift.
2. The system loads active deployed staff.
3. Uses **Mark all present** and changes only exceptions.
4. Adds authorized casual staff or an aggregate subcontractor crew where necessary.
5. Records hours and notes.
6. Saves the draft or confirms the register.

The page must be mobile-first, use a table rather than one card per worker, keep actions visible, and prevent horizontal page overflow.

### 7.5 Complete the DSR

The site engineer records labour by trade. The DSR displays attended person-hours, reported person-hours and variance next to each labour group. The project manager sees any exception before approval.

## 8. Pages and Navigation

Avoid a large nested HR menu.

- `/staff`: existing staff page, extended with employment type and primary trade.
- `/workforce`: one page with Deployments and Trades tabs.
- `/workforce/attendance`: attendance index with Draft and Confirmed tabs.
- `/workforce/attendance/create`: mobile attendance capture page.
- `/workforce/attendance/{register}`: attendance details and confirmation history.

Use one sidebar item named **Workforce** with Attendance as its primary destination. Trades and Deployments can be tabs or secondary actions from the Workforce page. Staff remains in its existing location to avoid duplicate worker registers.

## 9. Permissions and Policies

Minimum permissions:

- `workforce.view`
- `workforce.trades.manage`
- `workforce.deployments.manage`
- `workforce.attendance.record`
- `workforce.attendance.confirm`
- `workforce.attendance.reopen`
- `workforce.reports.view`
- `daily-site-reports.override-labour-variance`

Every policy must verify:

- tenant;
- branch access;
- project and site access;
- explicit permission;
- record status.

Attendance confirmation and reopening are permission based. They are not restricted merely because the same user recorded the attendance.

## 10. Audit and Notifications

Audit:

- trade creation, change, deactivation and restoration;
- staff employment type and trade changes;
- deployment creation and ending;
- attendance creation, editing, confirmation and reopening;
- DSR labour variance overrides.

Notifications:

- attendance still unconfirmed after the configured site deadline;
- DSR submitted with missing attendance;
- DSR labour exceeds confirmed attendance;
- confirmed attendance reopened after a related DSR was submitted or approved.

Notification keys must be stable and deduplicated.

## 12. Required Tests

- Tenant and branch isolation for every workforce record.
- Project/site access restrictions.
- Trade active/inactive behaviour.
- Deployment reassignment preserves history.
- Duplicate site/date/shift register is rejected.
- Confirmed attendance cannot be directly edited.
- Unauthorized confirmation and reopening return `403`.
- Reopening requires a reason and creates an audit event.
- Absent workers cannot have working hours.
- Subcontractor attendance belongs to an authorized subcontractor company.
- DSR person-hours match attendance.
- Under-reporting creates a warning.
- Over-reporting blocks ordinary approval.
- Authorized override requires a reason and is audited.
- Missing attendance warns without blocking the MVP workflow.
- Cost and sensitive data remain hidden without permission.

## 13. MVP Acceptance Criteria

The module is accepted when:

- Staff are assigned to real projects and sites without losing deployment history.
- A foreman can complete a site muster roll comfortably on a phone.
- Confirmed attendance cannot be silently changed.
- A DSR reviewer can see attended versus reported person-hours by trade.
- Unsupported excess labour cannot be approved without explicit authority and a reason.
- Subcontractor labour remains linked to the correct company.
- Direct unauthorized requests return `403`.
- Important actions appear in the audit trail.
- Demo data proves a matching case, an under-reported case and an overridden exception.
- Focused tests and the full repository quality suite pass.

## 14. Confirmed MVP Decisions

The implementation uses these decisions:

1. The MVP supports both day and night shifts.
2. The normal daily hours and allowed comparison tolerance.
3. Attendance abilities are controlled by permissions and may be assigned to any suitable role.
4. Labour-variance override is permission controlled and is not hard-coded to a role.
5. Subcontractor worker names are optional; aggregate company-and-trade crew counts are valid.

All other payroll, leave, biometric and personal-identity requirements remain deferred until the client validates a real need.
